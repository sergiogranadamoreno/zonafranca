BOGOTÁ MUSIC LAB & LEGAL FRAMEWORK

Redesigning the Independent Music Economy in the Global South

Challenging an industry standard where artists receive only 17% in royalties, ZONA FRANCA implements mandatory Split Sheets and 50/50 Net Profit Agreements embedded into metadata to guarantee 40% to 50% direct revenue and intellectual property for creators.

Listen to Audio Sample Download Dossier (PDF)
Traditional Model

17% Royalty

Payouts delayed behind opaque label cost recoupment. Loss of master ownership and payments blocked by "legal chaos".

ZONA FRANCA Model

40%–50% Direct

Binding Split Sheets, transparent cost deduction, 50/50 net profit splits, and self-authorizing ISRC metadata.

01 / Service & Legal Design

Economic Innovation & Metadata

Operating as a production laboratory and self-executing legal framework before a single note is recorded.

50/50 Net Profit Agreements

Total production costs are calculated with 100% transparency. Once direct costs are recouped, all net profits are split equally between the platform and collaborators.

Split Sheets & ISRC Metadata

Written, binding agreements signed prior to recording to define exact copyright shares for each artist, embedded directly into global ISRC metadata.

02 / Methodology

6-Stage Design Pipeline

01

Open Call (Ecosystem Design)

National open call selecting 25 diverse collaborators to guarantee territorial equity and inclusion.

02

Laboratory (Co-Creation & Design Sprint)

Collaboration between urban/electronic producers and traditional heritage keepers to prototype original compositions.

03

Recording & Production (Prototyping)

High-fidelity studio recording (24-bit WAV) in a professional environment.

04

Post-Production & Technical Sign-off (Legal Design)

Mastering, ISRC encoding, and formal execution of Split Sheets and Net Profit contracts.

05

Communications Strategy (Marketing & EPK)

Developing the Techno Tropical visual universe and assembling 30 dedicated Electronic Press Kits (EPKs).

06

Launch (Release & Distribution)

Commercial deployment across global streaming platforms (Spotify, Apple Music) and targeted promotional campaigns.
